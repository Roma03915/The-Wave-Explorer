TRAVEL SMART, TRAVEL KIND

Island notes

01

Before you go

Check weather advisories, transport schedules, local entry rules, and seasonal conditions before setting out.

02

Getting around

Confirm routes and fares with official transport providers or local tourism offices. Travel times can change with weather and traffic.

03

Leave it beautiful

Bring reusable essentials, respect wildlife and reef areas, follow local rules, and take your rubbish with you.
